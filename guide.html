Why diets feel so hard to stick to

Most of us were taught that weight comes down to willpower: eat less, move more, and the rest follows. Anyone who has tried knows it rarely feels that simple. Hunger comes back, cravings get louder, and the plan quietly slips.

That is not a character flaw. Appetite is driven by signals between your gut and your brain. Weight-loss medications work with those same signals, helping you feel satisfied sooner and quieting the constant pull toward food. For many people, it is the first time the effort they put in actually seems to land.

It is not about trying harder. It is about finally working with your body instead of against it.

Who these programs are for

Prescription weight-loss programs are designed for adults who want to lose weight with real medical support, especially people who have tried dieting on their own and found it did not last.

Many programs consider adults with a BMI of around 27 and above. The questionnaire takes care of the rest: a licensed clinician reviews your health history to make sure the plan is a good fit for you.
